02Featured research

Measuring what drives citation in AI search engines

My capstone asks whether Perplexity, Claude and OpenAI rank and cite sources consistently, and whether their rankings follow the rules of rational choice.

Capstone project, MS Data Science

Supervisor: Prof. George Hagstrom · CUNY SPS · 2026

A cross-engine pipeline that queries all three engines, extracts answer text, retrieval URLs and citation URLs, then tests stability and choice axioms with statistical models. It sits at the center of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): what makes a page get cited.

Test 1

Live-search stability

115 prompts, each run 5 times per engine. Jaccard similarity and rank-biased overlap across runs.

Test 2

Controlled document queues

48 web pages fetched with Playwright and trafilatura; 315 rankings each for long and short items.

Phase 3

Independence of irrelevant alternatives

Plackett-Luce worth estimates and a permutation null with 2,000 shuffles.

Phase 4

Transitivity

225 pairwise comparisons per engine; tie rates and conditional cycle rates.
